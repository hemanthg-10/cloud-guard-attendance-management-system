# Research references

These publications are included as background references for the project report. This repository links to the publisher/DOI pages rather than redistributing the PDFs.

1. Zhigang Gao et al., “A Student Attendance Management Method Based on Crowdsensing in Classroom Environment,” *IEEE Access*, 2021. [https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3060256](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3060256)
2. Viktor Erdélyi et al., “Detecting Subtle Signs of School Attendance Issues Using Smartphone-Based Sensing,” *IEEE Access*, 2025. [https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3523108](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3523108)
3. Hong-Danh Thai, Yeong-Seok Seo, and Jun-Ho Huh, “Enhanced Efficiency in SMEs Attendance Monitoring: Low Cost Artificial Intelligence Facial Recognition Mobile Application,” *IEEE Access*, 2024. [https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3504858](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3504858)

These papers discuss distinct research methods. The current Cloud Guard demo does not implement crowdsensing, phone-sensor screening, or face recognition.
